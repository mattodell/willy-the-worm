    Click or drag to paint · right-click (or Shift-drag) erases · keys 1–0 pick tools, X eraser, Ctrl/⌘+Z undo, Ctrl/⌘+S save the world, T test play the current level, G grid. Click a floor with an item tool (tack, present, spring, cannon, bell, Willy) and it lands on top of the floor. To remove an item, erase the item itself, not the floor under it. A ladder passes through a floor: put its top piece in the floor row so Willy can stand on it.
